New client checklist

  1. First call. Ask how many calls they miss and how they book jobs. Offer: they test it, pay nothing until happy.
  2. Send the setup form link. Their answers arrive in your Zoho email.
  3. Build their agent in Retell. Copy your demo agent, rename it to their business, paste in their answers.
  4. Test it yourself 3 or 4 times, including awkward questions.
  5. Let them test it with a web call. Fix what they don't like.
  6. Get paid. Once they approve, send the $497 Stripe link. Wait for the payment before spending anything.
  7. Go live. Buy a Retell number (about $2/mo), connect their agent, publish. Have them forward unanswered calls to it.
  8. Test call together to the live number.
  9. Check in after a week. Listen to a few recordings in Retell and adjust.
